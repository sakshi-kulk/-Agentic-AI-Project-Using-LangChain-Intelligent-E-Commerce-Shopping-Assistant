Python Programming Support
Windows and Linux laptops can run Python 3, common editors such as VS Code, and development tools after the customer installs them. Check operating-system compatibility and available storage before choosing a specific model.

Machine Learning Compatibility
Laptops with 16 GB RAM are suitable for learning Python, data analysis, and small introductory machine-learning projects. Large model training and GPU-intensive workloads generally need a dedicated GPU or cloud compute; integrated graphics are not a substitute for a workstation GPU.

Battery Details
Battery life varies with screen brightness, workload, battery age, and power settings. The catalog's battery-hour values are illustrative estimates and are not guaranteed runtime figures. Programming, video calls, and high performance settings can reduce battery backup.

Warranty Information
Warranty coverage depends on the exact model, seller, and purchase region. Confirm the manufacturer's warranty duration, service locations, accidental-damage coverage, and return policy on the product listing before purchase.
